# Stellar Lending Testnet Tester Guide

## Before you start

- Install [Freighter](https://freighter.app) and choose **Stellar Testnet**.
- Use a Testnet wallet only. Do not send mainnet funds.
- Never share your recovery phrase or secret key with anyone.

## Complete one interaction

1. Open the live app and choose **Start on Testnet**.
2. Connect Freighter.
3. Fund the public wallet with [Friendbot](https://friendbot.stellar.org).
4. Deposit a small amount of XLM on the Deposit page.
5. Optionally borrow dTOKEN, then repay or withdraw according to the displayed safety limit.
6. Wait for the confirmation screen and copy the transaction hash from Freighter or Stellar Expert.

## What to send the project team

- Your public Stellar Testnet wallet address.
- The confirmed transaction hash or Stellar Expert URL.
- The action you used (deposit, borrow, repay, withdraw, or liquidate).
- A rating from 1 to 10 and any short feedback.

For the 50-user cohort, testers should complete their confirmed interaction 2–3 minutes apart. The organiser will verify each public transaction before it is added to the evidence register.
